## Working Problem Definition

Problem: "There is no one ai powered fitness app for everything"

The group is investigating the idea of an AI-powered fitness app that combines strength training, cardio, diet, and overall fitness information.

Our early team experience suggests that gym goers have problems managing workout information and making decisions during a workout, such as finding previous results or adapting when equipment is busy.


## Affected People and Context

**Primary:** Gym goers who do or would like to track or follow workouts and other related fitness information.

**Secondary:** Personal trainers, commercial gyms, and group fitness instructors.

The main context is using fitness information before and during workouts, including situations where a person has limited time or equipment is unavailable.

## Most Important Next Question

**What specific problems do gym goers have with current fitness apps, and what do existing apps already solve?**

We also need to determine what AI could add beyond normal fitness tracking.

## Planned Stakeholder Test

**Stakeholder:** Gym goers who regularly work out or use fitness apps.

**Assumption to test:** Gym goers have difficulty managing workout information or adapting workouts in real gym situations, while tracking other fitness information in a single app.

**Rough prompt:**
*"Tell us about the last time a fitness app or your planned workout did not work the way you expected. What did you do instead?"*

**What we hope to learn:** Whether these problems occur outside our team, how people currently solve them, and whether there is a specific unmet need worth designing for.
